Lora Mattress Returns, Replacements & Refunds Policy

Please inspect your order when it is delivered. If the mattress or packaging appears damaged, or you receive the wrong product, take clear photos or a short video and contact us as soon as possible.

1. Damaged, defective or incorrect items

Please keep the mattress and its packaging while we review your report. We may ask for additional photos, a video, or an inspection to assess the issue.

If we confirm that the mattress was damaged before delivery, has a manufacturing defect, or is not the item you ordered, we’ll arrange an appropriate remedy in accordance with applicable law. This may include repair, replacement, or refund.

If collection is required, we’ll explain the arrangements. Please don’t transport the mattress to an outlet unless we’ve agreed to that in writing.

2. Change-of-mind returns

Because mattresses are large and difficult to handle, we’re unable to accept change-of-mind returns once a mattress has been delivered, unpacked, or used, except where required by law.

3. Comfort or preference concerns

We want you to love your Lora mattress! However, because mattresses are large and difficult to handle, we’re unable to accept returns or offer replacements if you simply prefer a different firmness or feel after delivery. Please review the product details and contact us with any questions before placing your order.

4. Refunds

Refunds will be provided where required by applicable law or where Lora approves a refund after reviewing a reported issue. Any approved refund will be made to the original payment method, subject to applicable law.

5. Contact us

Please include your order number, delivery date, a description of the issue, and any relevant photos or video.
