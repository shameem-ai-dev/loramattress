Sizing Guide

How to Measure Your Mattress

Follow these 3 simple steps to find the exact dimensions for your cot or bed frame.

STEP 01

Length

Length = top to bottom

Measure along the long edge from head to foot of the mattress.

STEP 02

Width

Width = side to side

Measure horizontally across the bed from one outer side to the other.

STEP 03

Thickness

Thickness = height of the mattress

Measure vertically from the bottom base tape-edge to the top sleeping crest.

Example Size: 72 × 60 × 6 inches
Measure your current bed or mattress before choosing a size.
